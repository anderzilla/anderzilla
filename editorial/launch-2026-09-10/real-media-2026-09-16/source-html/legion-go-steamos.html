Lenovo Levels Up CES with Legion Pro Rollable Concept and Latest Gaming Devices

Lenovo™ announced the newest additions to its portfolio of gaming devices designed to help esports athletes, student gamers, and mobile gamers.

6 January 2026

Las Vegas, US – January 6, 2026—Lenovo™ announced the newest additions to its portfolio of gaming devices designed to help esports athletes, student gamers, and mobile gamers. New for CES® 2026 are updates across Lenovo Legion and Lenovo LOQ devices, along with a new Legion laptop proof of concept featuring a rollable screen designed to provide discerning gamers with powerful, portable gaming devices to help them on their quest to “reach their impossible” in gameplay.

• The Legion Pro Rollable Concept is a 16” top-tier gaming laptop with a screen that expands horizontally from 16” to 21.5” to 24” for esports athletes who need to train for high-level competitions around the world.
• The Legion Go, Powered by SteamOS™ (8.8”, 2) is Lenovo Legion’s most powerful handheld to natively ship with SteamOS, combining the raw power of the Legion Go (8.8”, 2) with the freedom and game access of SteamOS.
• The Legion 7a features AMD’s newest Ryzen™ AI 400 Series processors and NVIDIA® GeForce RTX™ 50-series graphics for users who need a multitasking machine that can handle STEM apps, streaming, and AAA gaming.

“The gaming space is as exciting as it is vast—from bringing friends together across borders, to elite esports athletes to the top competitions in the world. Wherever and however a gamer chooses to engage in the space, Lenovo Legion has designed laptops, software, and accessories to enable them to succeed,” said Ouyang Jun, Senior Vice President, Consumer Segment, Lenovo Intelligent Devices Group. “Our newest Lenovo Legion devices are designed to meet gamers where they are—be it at the desk, on the road, or at the championship podium.”

Never Stop Training: The Lenovo Legion Pro Rollable (Concept)

The greatest esports athletes demand the highest-performance training equipment to prepare themselves for elite competitions around the world. Championship esports athletes compete on 24” and larger displays, and the most effective training replicates competition conditions, which can be difficult as a professional esports athlete travels the globe to compete. The Lenovo Legion Pro Rollable Concept was designed to answer the call from athletes who need pro-level performance in a device that can travel with them.

The Legion Pro Rollable concept features a rollable Lenovo PureSight OLED Gaming display that unrolls from both ends using a dual-motor, tension-based design that allows the display to expand and contract with minimal vibration and noise and translates to a smooth, controlled expansion and contraction experience. The dedicated tensioning system maintains consistent tautness across the OLED panel, while strategically applied low-friction materials protect the panel from abrasion throughout the rolling cycle.

The Lenovo PureSight OLED display starts at 16” in Focus Mode when athletes are refining precision mechanics and improving reflexes. When training peripheral awareness, rotation drills and team coordination, the screen expands to 21.5” in Tactical Mode. When it’s time to focus on elite competitive training and overall team cohesiveness, the screen can expand to a full 24” in Arena Mode, allowing professional esports athletes traveling around the world to carry small but train big.

The Legion Pro Rollable proof of concept is based on the Legion Pro 7i, with top-spec Intel® Core™ Ultra processors and an NVIDIA® GeForce RTX™ 5090 Laptop GPU. Powered by NVIDIA Blackwell, GeForce RTX™ 50 Series Laptop GPUs bring game-changing capabilities to gamers and creators. Equipped with a massive level of AI horsepower, the RTX 50 Series enables new experiences and next-level graphics fidelity. Multiply performance with NVIDIA DLSS 4, generate images at unprecedented speed, and unleash creativity with NVIDIA Studio.

The proof of concept also features Lenovo AI Engine+, powered by the Lenovo LA Core (LA1 + LA3), which features real-time Scenario Detection that boosts FPS and optimizes resources for peak performance. Smart FPS dynamically fine-tunes settings to ensure smooth, stable gameplay, even in high-pressure moments. AI-powered CPU and GPU tuning push FPS higher for top-tier competitive play.

Improving The Game: The AI Frame Gaming Display Proof of Concept

The AI Frame Gaming Display proof of concept features built-in AI functionalities that assist gamers in refining their fundamentals and helping them stay more aware of key visual information during play. The AI Frame Gaming Display features:

  • AI Scene Detection: Identifies the type of game being played in order to intelligently zoom in key areas and display them in the top-right corner of the screen, making map information clearer or highlighting relevant visual information during high-intensity gameplay—especially useful for FPS or MOBA games.
  • Cursor Tracking: This feature tracks where the cursor is on the screen and displays the zoomed-in picture at the top right of the display.
  • AI Game Assistance: Identifies game bosses and other difficult mission sections across a multitude of games and provides real-time guidance to help players progress through a game’s toughest challenges. The monitor will dynamically readjust to show both the game and a smaller window displaying the guidance.
  • Adaptive AI Lighting: Provides real-time atmospheric lighting that changes based on what is happening in-game. When the player is struck, for example, the Adaptive AI Lighting will change colors to indicate damage being taken.

The AI Frame Gaming Display showcases Lenovo’s commitment to improving the gamer’s experience, whether it’s beating the competition to top the leaderboards, or making sure the altimeter says 30,000 feet, not 300.

For Max Power, Just Press Play: The Legion Go, Powered by SteamOS (8.8”, 2)

The Legion Go, Powered by SteamOS (8.8”, 2) is Lenovo Legion’s most powerful handheld to natively ship with SteamOS, delivering desktop-class performance with console-like ease by forging the full power of the Legion Go (8.8”, 2) together with the effortless gaming experience of SteamOS.

Powered by up to an AMD Ryzen™ Z2 Extreme processor, up to 32GB1 LPDDR5X memory and with up to 2TB1 PCIe SSD storage (further expandable up to an additional 2TB1 via microSD), SteamOS is optimized to make full use of the device hardware, bringing even AAA titles to life on the 8.8-inch PureSight OLED display.

SteamOS offers a console-like experience designed for gamepad controls optimized to get into games quickly and effortlessly, with features like quick suspend/resume for easy entry and exit. SteamOS is built for plug-and-play gaming, with full access to a user’s Steam Library, seamless cloud saves, centralized notifications, Steam Chat, and built-in Game Recording. The full Steam Store is readily available for full access to the newest titles.

Master Your Many Worlds: The Legion 7a (16”, 11)

The Legion 7a (16”, 11) is a sleek and versatile powerhouse built for gamers, streamers, and innovators. 10% lighter and up to 5% thinner than its predecessor, the Windows 11 Copilot+ PC is designed to move with the user from one passion to the next. Whether driving new breakthroughs in STEM, streaming, creating, or gaming, the Legion 7a is the tool to make it happen.

Fueled by AMD’s brand new Ryzen™ AI 400 processors and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 50 Series Laptop GPUs delivering a total of 125W TDP, the Legion 7a (16”, 11) delivers responsive, AI-optimized performance across gaming, streaming, and complex technical projects such as coding, simulations, and 3D modeling. Lenovo AI Engine+ informs the Legion Space software suite how to best fine-tune power, thermals, and efficiency in real time, shifting smoothly from all-night gaming sessions to high-intensity lab work.

Legion Coldfront: Hyper cooling keeps heat in check even under heavy loads, while the brilliant Lenovo PureSight OLED Gaming display makes every detail pop with rich contrast, true color, and ultra-low latency.

Explore Your Many Sides: The Legion 5i (15”, 11) and Legion 5a (15”, 11)

The latest Legion 5 series is the do-it-all laptop built to game, create, stream, and deliver any project without missing a beat. Lighter, thinner, and built for life in motion, it moves easily from one world to the next. Available with either AMD Ryzen AI 400 Series processors in the Legion 5a (15”, 11) or Intel Core Ultra Series 3 processors in the Legion 5i (15”, 11), both Windows 11 Copilot+ PCs have NVIDIA GeForce RTX 50 Series Laptop GPUs and Lenovo AI Engine+, delivering AI-tuned power and high-FPS responsiveness whether jumping into a late-night gaming session, streaming gameplay, building presentations, or editing the next big project.

The vivid Lenovo PureSight OLED display brings every detail to life, while Legion Coldfront: Hyper cooling keeps things running quietly and efficiently behind the scenes even when the action heats up.

In addition, the Legion 5 series also includes a new Legion 5a (15”, 11), powered by AMD’s new Ryzen 200 Series processors.

Get Ready to Roar: The Lenovo LOQ 15AHP11 and Lenovo LOQ 15IPH11

The newest additions to the Lenovo LOQ series provide gamers with everything they need to dive into their gaming journeys. Available with either AMD Ryzen 200 Series processors or Intel Core Ultra processors, the Lenovo LOQ 15AHP11 and Lenovo LOQ 15IPH11 both feature NVIDIA GeForce RTX 50-Series Laptop GPUs, built for gamers and students who need a laptop that can handle coursework and gaming. Both laptops feature Hyperchamber Cooling technology that optimizes airflow over key components, efficiently expelling hot air away from the laptop and the user while reducing fan noise.

Both the LOQ 15AHP11 and LOQ 15IPH11 are available in Luna Grey with up to a 15.3” WQXGA display.

For more details about all Lenovo’s new devices, solutions, and proofs of concept announced at CES 2026, visit the Lenovo CES 2026 Press Kit.
