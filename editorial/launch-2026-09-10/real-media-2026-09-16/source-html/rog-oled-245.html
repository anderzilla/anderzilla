New 24.5-inch ROG monitors bring OLED panel tech to the esports arena

For professional gamers, the 24-inch monitor class stands as the gold standard. While many other gamers in the desktop space have moved on to larger models, hardcore competitors still tend to insist on more compact displays so that they can keep every pixel of the battlefield within view without turning their head. Unfortunately for the 24-inch monitor faithful, displays of this class have thus far been shut out of the OLED revolution. Even though OLED gaming monitors wield specs that seem purpose-built for esports, such as their world-beating response times, 24-inch OLED gaming monitors simply haven’t been an option.  

That era of compromise is ending. Back at Computex 2026, we gave you a sneak peek at our first 24.5-inch OLED gaming monitor, the ROG Strix OLED XG259QWPG Ace. Today, we’re announcing three more options. Our comprehensive lineup of 24.5-inch OLED gaming monitors brings the modern pinnacle of panel technology to the most demanding size in the industry, giving you plenty of options for upgrading your esports arsenal. 

LCD vs. OLED: How do these panel types compare for esports? 

For years, esports monitors almost exclusively wielded TN panels. Built for pure speed, these monitors made stratospheric refresh rates possible, but they struggled to shake off the limitations of the tech: washed-out colors, shallow contrast, and poor viewing angles, to name a few.  

The latest OLED panels kick those limitations to the curb. Here’s a quick comparison between the display technologies:

OLED panels offer head-turning visual quality, so they appeal to gamers of all kinds. There’s some discussion online about the value of things like inky blacks, vibrant colors, and professional-grade color accuracy from the perspective of esports gaming. Will they make your games more fun to play? Almost certainly. Will they bump up your K/D ratio? Maybe not. But here’s a quality of OLED panels that offers an inarguable competitive edge for esports gaming: incredible motion clarity.  

We dive deep into this topic in another article if you’d like to know more, but here’s the short version. All modern monitors have to battle motion blur to a certain extent, thanks to two engineering challenges. The first is pixel transition speed. Pixels can’t instantly switch between two colors. During transition from one color to another, you see a temporary blended state that can appear blurry. Second is what we call “sample-and-hold,” or the way that modern monitors display each frame statically until the next one arrives, with no “break” between frames. This causes your eyes to track a series of static images that instantly shift from one point to the next. As a result, you’ll always perceive a certain amount of blur in this scenario, even if there’s nothing blurry in the images themselves. 

OLED gaming monitors tackle these concerns in two ways. First is a generational leap forward in pixel transition speeds. Compare the response times of an OLED gaming monitor with an LCD, and the OLED’s response time will be an order of magnitude faster, if not two. All by itself, this upgrade means minimized blur in fast-paced action.  

As for sample-and-hold, there are a variety of strategies out there for countering this concern, like backlight strobing. OLED gaming monitors, however, can address this source of motion blur through sheer overwhelming speed. Grab a top-of-the-line OLED monitor with a 720Hz refresh rate, play a game that your system can run fast enough to take advantage of that 720Hz refresh rate, and you’ll be hard-pressed to spot motion blur of any kind. 

All told, the motion clarity on offer with OLED panels is off the charts, and that’s what’s so exciting about them for esports. When the margin for victory depends on spotting, tracking, and responding to fast-moving opponents in impossibly small windows of time, you need every available weapon in your arsenal.

Series-wide upgrades for competitors 

Before we dig into the differences between the three models, some common features across the series. 

Professionals rely on carefully arranged setups, but don’t always have a lot of time to line everything up exactly in tournament conditions. Every ROG Ace OLED monitor includes integrated measurement scales on the tilt (-5°–+33°) and height (0–155mm) adjustments to help players consistently reproduce their preferred setup. The Quick OSD menu lets players easily tweak core settings before every game. Different games can call for different display settings, so we’re making it easy for pros to quickly dial things in. Three Esports Color modes provide tailored presets optimized for three different FPS gaming scenarios.  

There’s a key upgrade under the hood, too. Every ROG Ace OLED monitor incorporates Gallium Nitride Field-Effect Transistor (GaNFET) technology in its power-supply design. The elevated efficiency of GaNFET tech helps to optimize heat dissipation, delivering up to 35% less waste heat and a 10% lower vent temperature. That increased efficiency allowed us to optimize the displays for excellent brightness as well as a long-running product lifespan.   

Four ROG Ace OLED gaming monitor options for your esports setup 

Between the three options, you’ll have your pick between two panel types, three refresh rates, and three price points, among other considerations. Standing at the top of the winner’s podium is the ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace. 

ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace 

The most aggressive display ever engineered for the 24.5-inch monitor class, the PG259QWS ACE pushes the boundaries of human perception with a 720Hz refresh rate and a near-instantaneous 0.02ms response time. These premium specs allow it to deliver a level of fluidity and visual punch that used to be purely theoretical. The action happens at Full HD (1920x1080), a GPU-friendly resolution that’ll let your modern gaming rig run esports titles at many hundreds of FPS. The Tandem WOLED panel and TrueBlack Glossy™ coating are perfect for gaming under the bright lights of a tournament, as they deliver high perceptual brightness and excellent contrast even in high ambient lighting conditions. 

This monitor is a standout choice for HDR gaming, as well. It’s capable of jaw-dropping 1700-nit highlights. It also supports Dolby Vision, which means you get more dynamic HDR visuals in supported content tuned perfectly for the display’s capabilities.  

All the ROG Ace OLED monitors allow for quick setup changes on the tournament stage, but the PG259QWS Ace takes things to the next level. Our exclusive Position Sensor uses a built-in sensor to measure the monitor's tilt and height angles and display them on-screen, giving pro players the information they need to quickly and precisely dial in their exact preferred setup even in high-pressure environments. It even features a tripod socket, a welcome quality-of-life addition for streaming setups. 

You won’t have to wait long to see the ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace under the bright lights. It’s already been named as the official esports monitor of the 2026 PGL Major tournament, where it’ll bring the world's fastest OLED tech to the sport's biggest stage. 

ROG Strix OLED XG259QDPG Ace 

The ROG Strix OLED XG259QDPG Ace pivots to a 560Hz RGB QD-OLED panel. (Side note: if you need to catch up with all the new OLED panel types, click here for an in-depth comparison.) We equip this panel with BlackShield™ Film and an anti-glare low-reflection coating. Not only does this boost perceived black levels by up to 40% in brighter lighting conditions, but it also keeps reflections under control and increases panel hardness by 2.5X for easier cleaning.  

ROG Strix OLED XG259QDNS Ace  

Out of the three options we’re announcing today, the ROG Strix OLED XG259QDNS Ace will offer the most mainstream price tag. Featuring a vivid RGB QD-OLED panel, BlackShield Film, and an anti-glare low-reflection coating, it’s a great fit for most PC gaming setups. The refresh rate isn’t quite as stratospheric as with the other two models, but there’s nothing slow about its 360Hz refresh rate. This monitor will enable a wider base of players to enjoy Ace-grade esports visuals and pro-tuned setup tools. 

ROG Strix OLED XG259QWPG Ace  

Perhaps you’re looking for a WOLED gaming monitor from our mainstream ROG XG display lineup. The ROG Strix OLED XG259QWPG Ace, which we unveiled back at Computex 2026, is the monitor you’re looking for. Tandem WOLED technology lets this display deliver higher brightness and a longer lifespan compared to conventional WOLED panels. Its 540Hz refresh rate is certified fresh for esports. That raw speed, along with the stunningly low 0.02ms response time (GTG), all but banishes the specter of motion blur.  

ROG 24.5-inch OLED monitors arrive to shake up the esports scene  

To battle with the best, you have to play with the best. And that’s as true of your hardware as it is of your teammates. The latest additions to the ROG Ace lineup bring the latest advancements in OLED technology in esports-ready 24.5-inch designs. If you’ve been waiting for a tournament-grade OLED monitor to hit the scene, your wait is just about over. Stay tuned for more information on the pricing and availability of these monitors.
