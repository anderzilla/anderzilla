Pre-order your ROG XBOX Ally X20 today, standalone or as part of an incredible ROG 20th anniversary bundle

The long wait is finally over. Pre-orders are now open for the ROG XBOX Ally X20, our incredible new gaming handheld. With a stunning new gold and black design, an upgraded 7.4-inch OLED screen, and control improvements across the board, it's a physical representation of 20 years of ROG gaming innovation. It’s available standalone for $1,299, or as part of a special ROG 20th Anniversary Bundle for $2,499 featuring the ROG XREAL R1 Edition 20 Gaming AR Glasses, the dbrand Killswitch for ROG XBOX Ally X20, and a custom Pelican Protector Case. No matter which loadout you choose, this special edition handheld is ready to play anywhere.

Incredible 20th Anniversary styling and performance

Designed from the ground up to celebrate a 20-year legacy in gaming, the ROG XBOX Ally X20 is an instant showstopper. Featuring a semi-translucent black chassis that harkens back to earlier eras of gaming, it also offers a glimpse of the gold-accented internal structure and custom gold-plated fans. Complete with custom gold lighting around the joysticks, a backlit green XBOX button, and a custom gold-plated ROG 20th Anniversary badge on the back, this is the Ally that people will be talking about for years to come.

Equipped with a stunning 7.4-inch ROG Nebula HDR Display with an OLED panel and powered by the latest AMD Ryzen AI Z2 Extreme processor, the ROG XBOX Ally X20 is the best gaming handheld we've ever built. With a VESA True Black 1400 certification and full FreeSync Premium Pro support with an expanded VRR range of 30Hz-120Hz, your games will look more immersive than ever on this display. 1400 nits of peak brightness create an HDR image that pops right off the screen, while an anti-reflection coating cuts unwanted environmental reflections by 65% to let you stay focused on the action.

We've learned from years of experience across the ROG Ally lineup, and given you even more precise control with the X20. The familiar, controller-esque grips give you comfort on the go, while a new textured rubberized coating provides a more secure hold as you play. Magnetic TMR joysticks reduce the deadzone to 3% and come with a new algorithm that eliminates four-axis snapping, all with minimal power draw. A new hybrid rubber/metal domed transforming D-pad lets you easily switch from 4-way precision in indie games to the 8-way freedom that fighting games demand. The face buttons have also been tweaked and now sit more flush with the chassis when fully depressed to let your thumbs more easily glide between inputs.

Unlock new ways to play with the ultimate bundle

For the discerning collector, we've also created an incredible bundle to celebrate ROG's 20th. It includes the ROG XBOX Ally X20, the ROG XREAL R1 Edition 20 Gaming AR Glasses, the dbrand Killswitch for ROG XBOX Ally X20, and a custom Pelican Protector Case to house it all in.

The ROG XREAL R1 Edition 20 Gaming AR Glasses feature the same iconic black-and-gold colorway found on the XBOX Ally X20. These next-level AR glasses utilize a pair of 1080p Micro-OLED displays to create a virtual 171-inch screen at 4 meters, covering 95% of your focused viewing area for incredible immersion. Powered and connected to your ROG XBOX Ally X20 with just a single USB-C cable, the ROG XREAL R1 Edition 20 Gaming AR Glasses let you carry next-level immersion wherever you go. Both are available for pre-order as standalone devices starting today.

The bundle also includes a custom Killswitch case from dbrand, with an included skin patterned after the internal layout of the XBOX Ally X20’s motherboard. dbrand also designed custom joystick covers to give you more grip during intense gaming sessions, and a travel cover to protect your device on the go.

But for the ultimate in protection, the bundle also includes a custom Protector Case from Pelican, which includes a foam interior perfectly sized for your Ally, glasses, and a controller of your choice. You can rest easy knowing that your device will always arrive safe, no matter where you’re headed. Patterned in a special black-and-gold Edition 20 colorway, this is the ultimate travel or display case.

Available for pre-order today

The ROG XBOX Ally X20 Bundle, ROG XBOX Ally X20 and the ROG XREAL R1 Edition 20 Gaming AR Glasses are all available for pre-order today. The complete bundle is available for $2,499, the ROG XBOX Ally X20 standalone is available for $1,299, and the ROG XREAL R1 Edition 20 Gaming AR Glasses standalone is available for $799. The ROG XBOX Ally X20 will be on sale starting on October 15th, and the ROG XREAL R1 Edition 20 Gaming AR Glasses will be on sale in mid-September. 

Final pricing and availability may vary depending on the region and local market. For more information, please reach out to your local ROG representative.
