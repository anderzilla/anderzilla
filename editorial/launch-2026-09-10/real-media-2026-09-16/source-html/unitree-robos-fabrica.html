Year of 2026

On February 13, Unitree unveiled the UnifoLM-X1-0 embodied intelligence model and deployed it for testing at its own robotics manufacturing facility, enabling robots to assemble and manufacture other robots.

On February 16, Unitree delivered a spectacular performance of *WuBot* at China Media Group’s Year of the Horse Spring Festival Gala, featuring 24 G1 humanoid robots and one H2 humanoid robot. Dozens of G1 robots completed the world’s first fully autonomous group martial arts performance by humanoid robots, including rapid formation changes and high-speed repositioning. The performance pushed the limits of robotic motion and set several global firsts. H2 also made a striking appearance at both the Beijing main venue and the Yiwu sub-venue, clad in Monkey King-inspired heavy armor and riding a B2-W robotic dog styled as the Monkey King’s somersault cloud to deliver festive blessings.

On February 24, Unitree launched the As2 quadruped robot. Despite its compact form factor, As2 delivers industry-leading performance, including a peak torque of 90 N·m, more than four hours of unloaded battery life, IP54 protection against rain and water splashes, and over 2.5 hours of continuous operation while carrying a 15 kg payload. It can also travel continuously for more than 13 kilometers. With an open secondary-development ecosystem, As2 is designed to support a broad range of industrial applications.

On February 26, Unitree was honored to welcome the German Chancellor and his delegation. Unitree looks forward to collaborating with more outstanding companies around the world to advance intelligent robotics and ensure that technological progress benefits humanity.

On March 5, Unitree open-sourced the UnifoLM-WBT-Dataset, a high-quality, real-world full-body teleoperation dataset for humanoid robots in open environments. The dataset will be updated frequently on a rolling basis, with the goal of building a real-world humanoid robotics dataset featuring the broadest possible range of scenarios, the highest level of task complexity, and the greatest diversity of operations.

On April 11, Unitree’s H1 humanoid robot exceeded a top running speed of 10 m/s, once again setting a new world record.

On April 23, Unitree’s G1 humanoid robot demonstrated multiple forms of locomotion, including wheeled movement, roller skating, and ice skating, further expanding the boundaries of robotic mobility.

On May 12, Unitree unveiled GD01, the world’s first mass-produced rideable mech. Weighing approximately 500 kg with an operator onboard, GD01 can freely switch between bipedal and quadrupedal configurations, generating widespread attention worldwide.

On May 19, Unitree demonstrated the ability to control G1 directly through external voice commands, with AI generating a wide range of movements in real time.

On May 25, Unitree released the WVLA 2.0 model, demonstrating strong performance in organizing a cluttered meeting room under significant external disturbances.

On June 19, Myanmar President Min Aung Hlaing and his delegation visited Unitree to learn about and exchange views on the company’s latest robotic products and technological achievements. Unitree looks forward to working with more partners worldwide to accelerate the adoption of robotics across industries and bring intelligent robotic technologies into everyday life.

2021

On 11th February 2021, 24 sets A1, in the form of ox, brilliantly landed at CCTV Spring Festival Gala of the Year of the Ox to perform together with Andy Lau, Yibo Wang, Xiaotong Guan. This is the current human top foot robot technology and the deep integration of China's traditional zodiac culture, It is also the world's quadruped robot dynamic group dance performance, which has aroused heated discussion.

In June 2021, the Concomitant Bionic Robot Go1 was released, which became the first mobile robot in the history of human science and technology to truly enter the public life with its ultra-low price and excellent sensory movement ability that broke through the industry limit. Go1 has a maximum running speed of 4.7m/s (17Km/h), successfully breaking the world record for the fastest running of a quadruped robot of similar specifications. The standard configuration is the self-developed ISS intelligent accompanying system, which adopts patented wireless vector positioning and control technology, and SSS super sensing system. It relies on 5 sets of fish-eye binocular depth sensing + fish-eye AI sensing to achieve super sensing perspective coverage. The integrated and collaborative work of ISS and SSS can realize all-terrain autonomous obstacle avoidance and human body automatic follow-up, making human-computer interaction more natural, harmonious, safe, and secure; at the same time, it has built-in 16-core CPU+GPU super AI computing power. The release of Go1 officially opens a new era of concomitant mobile robots.

In June 2016, Wang Xingxing's robot test video released during his master's degree and after his graduation defense was reported by many media at home and abroad. Wang Xingxing, who was still working in Shenzhen at the time, decided to seize the opportunity, resigned to start a business, and founded the current robot company through angel investment.

2016-2013

During his master's degree, based on understanding foreign literature, Wang Xingxing, the current CEO of Unitree Robotics, independently developed the XDog, a full-degree-of-freedom high-powered quadruped robot driven by a low-cost external rotor brushless motor (X means mystery). The development of the entire first-generation product includes the simulation verification of quadruped robot control, the selection of permanent magnet synchronous motors, the design of motor drive boards and the development of driver programs, the design of master-slave control architecture, single-leg design and testing, development, and test of the control program. This low-cost solution has now become the most mainstream quadruped robot development direction in the world.
