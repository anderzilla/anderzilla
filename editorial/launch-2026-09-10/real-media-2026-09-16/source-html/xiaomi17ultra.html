Xiaomi 17 Ultra FAQ

Product Information

Q1: What series does the Xiaomi 17 Ultra belong to?

A: The Xiaomi 17 Ultra belongs to the Xiaomi series.
 

Q2: What are the selling points of the Xiaomi 17 Ultra?

A: The selling points of the Xiaomi 17 Ultra are as follows:
1. The Xiaomi 17 Ultra features a Leica 200MP 75–100 mm telephoto camera.
2. It is equipped with a Leica 1-inch ultra-dynamic main camera.
3. It features Leica UltraPure optical design for purer and clearer imaging.
4. It packs a 6000 mAh (typ) Xiaomi Surge battery for long-lasting endurance.
5. It is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 Mobile Platform for flagship-level performance.
6. It supports Xiaomi HyperAI for a smarter using experience.
The special selling points for the Xiaomi 17 Ultra Leica Version:
1. It features the essential Leica design, including Leica industrial design, Leica UX/UI, and a Leica camera ring, on the Xiaomi 17 Ultra Leica Version.
2. It is equipped with a Leica triple camera system, including a Leica 200MP 75–100 mm telephoto, a Leica 1-inch ultra-dynamic main camera, and a Leica UltraPure optical design.
3. It enables original Leica photography styles, such as Original Leica Looks, Signature Leica Bokeh, Leica Essential Mode, and Leica Frames.
4. It delivers a flagship experience with an ultra-bright display and a 6000 mAh (typ) Xiaomi Surge battery.
5. It is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 Mobile Platform for outstanding performance.
6. It supports Xiaomi HyperAI to provide smarter and more intelligent photography and system interaction.

Q1: What are the size and weight of the Xiaomi 17 Ultra?
