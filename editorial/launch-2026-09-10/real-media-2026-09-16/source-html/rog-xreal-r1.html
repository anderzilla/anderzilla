ROG XREAL R1

ROG XREAL R1 Gaming AR Glasses – 240Hz refresh rate, 0.01ms response time, Micro-OLED display, 57° FOV, Electrochromic Dimming, Native 3DoF (Anchor & Follow Mode), Instant 3D, HDR (via ROG Control Dock), ROG ecosystem connectivity with ROG Ally, Sound by Bose

  • 240Hz Refresh Rate with 0.01ms Response Time: Micro‑OLED FHD (1920 × 1080) gaming glasses deliver ultra‑low latency and exceptionally smooth, responsive gameplay
  • 57° Expanded Field of View for Immersive Visuals: Projects a 171‑inch virtual screen at 4 meters, covering 95% of the focused viewing area for a truly immersive experience
  • Native 3DoF with Electrochromic Lenses: Supports Anchor Mode and Follow Mode. Anchor Mode enables automatic transparency, while three manually adjustable electrochromic tint levels provide visual comfort across different lighting conditions.
  • Electrochromic Lens Technology: 3 tint levels to adapt to changing lighting conditions for optimal visual comfort.
  • Instant 3D: Transforms standard 2D content into immersive 3D effect for enhanced depth and realism
  • Audio Sound by Bose: Enhances spatial awareness and deepens in‑game immersion with rich, detailed sound
  • Seamless Connected & Controlled: USB‑C plug‑and‑play connectivity with deep ROG Ally integration, allowing Ally to directly control the glasses.
  • ROG Control Dock Compatibility: Features HDMI® and DisplayPort™ connectivity to ensure seamless compatibility with PCs and gaming consoles

The ROG XREAL R1 is a wearable gaming AR glasses engineered for PC, console, and ROG Ally players who demand total immersion. Experience a massive, fluid viewing experience in a lightweight 91g form factor, featuring a high-performance 171-inch virtual Micro-OLED screen with an industry-leading 240Hz refresh rate and 0.01ms response time—the ultimate portable display alternative for first-person ARPGs and high-speed racing games.

As a core part of the ROG Ecosystem, the R1 offers unparalleled synergy across your devices. A single USB-C connection provides a seamless, plug-and-play experience with the ROG Ally handheld, while the ROG Control Dock acts as a central hub for your stationary setup, letting you instantly switch between gaming consoles and your PC. With native 3DoF technology, you can pin your screen in space or let it follow your movements, while advanced electrochromic lens technology supports automatic transparency for perfect visual clarity in any lighting condition. Feature Sound by Bose audio and Instant 3D viewing; the ROG XREAL R1 gaming AR glasses redefine big-screen gaming, whether you’re at home or on the go.

Ergonomic Design For Comfort

At 91 grams, the R1 is balanced for immediate comfort. Featuring adjustable nose pads and flexible temples, it provides a stable, personalized fit, maintaining a natural feel in any environment.

Flexible Fit: Multi-Size Nose Pads

Includes M and L sizes to accommodate different nose bridge heights and facial shapes.

Adjustable Fit: Multi-Angle Temples

The temples support three levels of vertical angle adjustment, with up to 3.5° of tilt per level, allowing users to finetune the fit based on nose bridge height and ear position. A spring hinge design automatically adapts to different head sizes, providing a comfortable and secure fit with a total adjustment range of up to 15°.

Customized Fit

Tailor your viewing experience with adjustable IPD for perfect screen alignment and reduced eye strain. R1 comes in two IPD sizes, covering 95% of consumers(from 57 and to 75 mm). Combined with custom prescription lens support, the R1 ensures every user enjoys a sharp, personalized immersive gaming experience without compromising clarity.

  • Understanding IPD & Finding Yours

    Interpupillary Distance (IPD) is the space between your pupils. Precise IPD adjustment is key to clear visuals and accurate depth perception in AR. You can measure it via apps, at an optician, or with a ruler. Calibrating your R1 to this exact measurement ensures a sharp, centered view.

    Find Your IPD

  • Prescription Ready: Clear Vision for Every Gamer

    For users with myopia or astigmatism, the R1 offers a seamless vision solution. Each box includes a lens insert for custom fitting at your local optician. For added convenience, you can order pre-fitted prescription lenses directly from the official store or authorized partners.

  • Hardware

    • The box includes a non prescription insert that can be fitted with prescription lenses by your optician.
      You can also order prescription lens inserts directly from the link below for a more convenient solution. (Link: www.xreal.com/prescription-lens-ROG-XREAL-R1)

    • The R1 is designed with eye comfort as a top priority. Unlike smartphones or laptops viewed at close range, the R1’s optical design projects images at a longer focal distance, like a projector. This approach is intended to reduce eye strain during extended use. Furthermore, the glasses have received the TÜV Eye Comfort certification, ensuring they meet rigorous standards for display quality and user health.
      To maintain optimal eye health, we recommend taking a 10-minute break for every 45 to 60 minutes of continuous use. Avoid using glasses in environments where you cannot maintain situational awareness to ensure your safety.

    • The R1 features 3-level electrochromic dimming technology, allowing you to adjust the lens transparency with a single button. Whether you are in a brightly lit cafe or outdoors, you can darken the lenses to ensure a clear, high-contrast viewing experience.

    • The glasses do not have a built-in battery. Power is supplied directly by the connected host device.
      Power consumption is less than 3W(or less than 7.5W when used with a dock), ensuring efficient operation with minimal impact on the host device’s battery life.

    • The R1 features a Sound by Bose audio system with spatial audio support for an immersive experience.
      Its directional audio design is engineered to minimize sound leakage and protect your privacy. However, for complete privacy or in quiet environments, using headphones is still recommended.

    • When boosting 240Hz, certain image processing algorithms are used to achieve a higher refresh rate, which may result in a slight reduction in text clarity.

    • Currently, the cable is fixed at 1.2 meters to guarantee the stability required for high refresh rate gaming.

  • Compatibility & Docking

    • Yes. Supports any device with USB-C DP Alt Mode, including ROG Ally, iPhone 15/16, and Android flagships. No external power is required.

    • Yes, by using the ROG Control Dock, you can connect devices via HDMI 2.0 or DisplayPort 1.4, such as the PS5, Xbox, or desktop PCs.

    • The glasses require external power, so the Nintendo Switch must be used with its Dock.
      By connecting the Dock to the ROG Control Dock via HDMI and supplying external power, the R1 can display the Switch output.

    • It depends on your hardware. While the ROG Ally and gaming PCs support high refresh rates, some devices (like the Nintendo Switch and phones) are capped at 60Hz.

    • The R1 functions as an external display, making it compatible with any game that runs on your connected device (e.g., ROG Ally, smartphone, PC, or console).

  • Software

    • No, you can use the glasses at any time without installing a dedicated app.

    • The R1 is equipped with a built-in X1 spatial processing chip, which enables 3DoF functionality directly on the glasses. As a result, Anchor Mode can be activated directly on the device, delivering a stable viewing experience without the discomfort or dizziness typically caused by the image moving with head motion.

  • Before You Buy

    • To ensure optimal visual clarity and wearing comfort, we recommend selecting the size based on your IPD (Interpupillary Distance):

      While these guidelines fit most users, everyone’s face shape is different. If you’re between sizes or uncertain, we highly recommend visiting a retail store to try both for the most accurate fit.
